Fresh Track is an interactive media campaign we're making for the **City of Ottawa** through the City Studio program. Our client is the city's transportation planning team. The goal is to raise awareness of Ottawa's bike paths and get more people riding them.

## The brief

The class split into four teams, and each team picked one user group to focus on. Our team, Fresh Track, is targeting **both visitors and locals**. Visitors often don't know the paths exist, and plenty of locals live near a path and still don't use it.

The team lead role rotates by project phase, so everyone gets a turn running the team.

## My parts

This is a team of four, so here is exactly what I did:

- **Background research** in the early phase, setting up the context for the campaign.
- **Print materials** in production, including the campaign poster.
- **Campaign copy**, the messaging used across the ads.
- **Team lead** for the final phase, including putting together our final presentation deck.

## Process

We worked like a real agency team: research and strategy first, then a low fidelity prototype, then production. Tasks were tracked on a Kanban board split into sprints, and we kept our research in one shared place so anyone could find it.

For the poster and copy, I kept the voice friendly and simple. The message isn't "you should cycle more." It's closer to showing people that a nice route is already right there, and inviting them to try it.

## What I learned

Working with a real client changes everything. Every design choice has to answer to the client's goal, not just to what looks good. It was also my first time leading a team through the final phase of a project, when the pressure is highest and everything has to come together.
