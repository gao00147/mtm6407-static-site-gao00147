# About me

I'm Yijia Gao, a designer and front-end developer living in Ottawa. I'm in my final term of Interactive Media Design at Algonquin College and I graduate in December 2026.

## Design first, then code

My background is in visual communication. I studied it at Luxun Academy of Fine Arts and then worked for a few years as a web and visual designer. Over time I got tired of designing screens and then waiting to see how someone else would build them. So I came to Canada to learn the development side too.

Now I like being the person who can do both. I can design a layout in Figma, then open VS Code and build it, and I understand what's easy and what's hard on each side. That's the kind of role I'm looking for after graduation: a hybrid designer and developer.

## My palette

My personal brand uses a **Morandi palette**: low saturation, low brightness colours that feel calm and a little bit vintage. It's the palette you're looking at on this site.
